PinLocate

Save the exact location of the people you visit: the pin, a landmark note and a photo of the house or gate. Share it to WhatsApp with Waze and Google Maps links, so anyone can find the door.

  • Pin where you are standing, or paste a Google Maps or Waze link.
  • Add a landmark note, such as “green gate next to the surau”, and photos of the gate.
  • Share a place to WhatsApp in one tap. Family can open it in Waze or Google Maps, or save it in their own PinLocate.
  • With Pro: plan a visit list for the day, keep tags, fields and visit history, and export to a spreadsheet.
  • No account and no ads. Your places stay on your phone.
