Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

PinLocate saves the locations of the people you visit: a pin, a landmark note and a photo of the house or gate. This policy explains what happens to that information, in the app and on this website.

The short version

  • PinLocate has no server and no account. Your people, places, notes and photos are stored only on your phone.
  • We, the developer, never receive your data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, in the app or on this website.
  • Data leaves your phone only when you share it, when you back it up, or in the few cases listed below.

What PinLocate stores on your phone

  • People you add: their names, and, if you link a phone contact, that contact's ID and phone number.
  • Places: coordinates, how accurate the GPS fix was, label, landmark note, and any tags and fields you add.
  • Photos you choose for a place. They are compressed and their EXIF data, including any GPS location, is removed.
  • Visits you log, with their dates and notes, and your current visit list.
  • App settings, such as your language.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

When data leaves your phone

When you share or navigate

  • Share card: when you tap Share, the place's name, coordinates, note and photo go to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp. Unless you turn it off in Settings, the card ends with a PinLocate link that holds the place's name and coordinates (see The PinLocate link below). Before your first share, PinLocate reminds you that the card shows a home.
  • Navigate: the coordinates go to Waze or Google Maps, or to their website if the app is not installed.

When you read a map link

  • Short links: a link such as maps.app.goo.gl is opened once over the internet, so PinLocate can read the location it points to. Only the link itself is sent, to Google or Waze.
  • Place name lookup: when a Google Maps link has a place name but no coordinates, the name and address in the link are looked up with your phone's built-in geocoder, which sends them to Google.

Backups and exports you make

  • Backup file: Save backup or Share backup creates one .zip with all your people, places, notes and photos. It is not encrypted. It goes only where you save or send it.
  • CSV (Pro): a spreadsheet of people, phone numbers, places, notes, tags and fields, without photos. It is not encrypted and goes only where you save or send it.

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android backs up PinLocate's database (people, places, notes and settings, but not photos) to your own Google account. Android encrypts this backup with your screen lock, and PinLocate allows it only when that encryption is available. When you move to a new phone with a cable or Wi-Fi transfer, the photos are copied too. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

Purchases

PinLocate Pro is a subscription bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; PinLocate only learns whether your subscription is active, and remembers that answer on your phone so Pro keeps working offline. Nothing about your purchase is sent to the developer. You can cancel anytime in Google Play.

The PinLocate link

A shared place can carry a link such as pinlocate.link/p#v=1&lat=…&lng=…&n=…. The place's coordinates and name sit after the #. Browsers never send that part to a website, so it never reaches the server.

  • With PinLocate installed, the link opens the app, which checks the coordinates and name and offers to save the place. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Without the app, the link opens a page on this website. The page reads the place from the link inside your browser and shows its name, with buttons for Waze, Google Maps and Google Play. Those buttons send the coordinates only to the service you tap.

This website

pinlocate.link has no analytics, cookies, trackers, or scripts or fonts from other websites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and the address of the page you open (without the part after #), and may keep them in its logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. The developer does not see these logs.

Deleting your data

Delete a person or place in the app and it is removed from your phone, together with its photos. Uninstalling PinLocate deletes everything it stored. Backup and CSV files you saved or sent elsewhere are yours to delete. Android backups in your Google account are managed in your Google account settings.

Children

PinLocate is not directed at children under 13.
